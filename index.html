What is Shape Score?

Think you can draw a perfect circle or a straight-sided triangle? Shape Score tests it.

You draw each shape with one finger, lift it when you are done, and see how close you came. Then try again and beat your best.

How to play

  1. Choose a shapePick the shape you want to draw.
  2. Draw it with your fingerTrace the shape on the screen in one stroke.
  3. Lift your fingerLifting your finger finishes the drawing.
  4. Get your scoreSee how accurate your shape was, then challenge yourself to do better.

What you get

Touch controls
Draw directly on your screen. There is nothing to learn before you start.
Multiple shapes
Try different shapes and find out which ones you draw best.
A score for every drawing
Each attempt is scored, so you can see exactly how accurate you were.
Improve with practice
Your hand gets steadier the more you play. Use your scores to aim for better results.
Quick to play
A round takes seconds, so it fits any short break.
