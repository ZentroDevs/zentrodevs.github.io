What is Shape Score?

Shape Score is a fitness app that turns how your body looks and measures into one clear number.

Instead of guessing from the mirror or reacting to daily changes on the scale, you get a steady score you can follow week after week.

How it works

  1. Add your detailsEnter the basics the app asks for. It only takes a minute.
  2. See your scoreShape Score shows your result as a single number with a short explanation.
  3. Track your progressCome back regularly and watch your score move as your routine pays off.

What you get

One clear number
No charts to decode. Your score is easy to read and easy to remember.
Progress over time
See how your score changes across days and weeks, so you know what is working.
Simple and focused
No clutter and no complicated setup. Open the app, check your score, get on with your day.
Made for everyday fitness
Whether you are just starting or already training, the score gives you a steady reference point.
